# Intellij PDF Viewer Plugin Changelog

## 2.0.1
FusionPDF - A Seamless PDF Reading Experience
FusionPDF is a lightweight and discreet PDF preview and reading plugin for IntelliJ IDEA and other IDEs. Simply drag and drop your PDF file into the IDE, and FusionPDF will instantly display it with a background that seamlessly blends with your current IDEA theme. No more distracting original PDF backgrounds – just a clean, comfortable reading experience tailored to your IDE's appearance.
Whether you're reviewing documentation, reading papers, or just sneaking in some reading while coding, FusionPDF ensures your PDF reading remains low-profile, letting you focus on your work without interruption.
